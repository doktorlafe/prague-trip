# Packing Checklist

## Documents
- [ ] Passport / ID
- [ ] Travel insurance
- [ ] Flight/train tickets, hotel confirmations
- [ ] Payment cards + some cash (CZK)

## Clothing
- [ ] Comfortable walking shoes (lots of cobblestones)
- [ ] Weather-appropriate layers (check forecast closer to dates — season TBD)
- [ ] Light rain jacket/umbrella

## Electronics
- [ ] Phone charger
- [ ] EU-style Type E/C power adapter (if coming from outside continental Europe)
- [ ] Portable battery pack

## Other
- [ ] Reusable water bottle
- [ ] Day bag/backpack for sightseeing
- [ ] PID Lítačka app installed for public transport tickets
