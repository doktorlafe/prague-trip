# Itinerary

Dates and number of days are **TBD** — this is a flexible framework (sketched for ~4–5 days) to adjust once dates are confirmed. Fill in specifics as they firm up.

## Day 1 — Old Town & Castle District
- Prague Castle complex (St. Vitus Cathedral, Golden Lane, Old Royal Palace)
- Charles Bridge
- Lesser Town (Malá Strana) — Lennon Wall, Kampa Island
- Dinner: traditional Czech restaurant (e.g. Lokál)

## Day 2 — Old Town & Jewish Quarter
- Old Town Square, Astronomical Clock
- Jewish Quarter (Josefov) — Old Jewish Cemetery, synagogues
- Municipal House, Powder Tower
- Evening: beer garden (Riegrovy sady or Letná)

## Day 3 — Prague 7 (Holešovice / Letná)
- Full day / half-day in the Holešovice–Letná district
- See [`prague-7-guide.md`](prague-7-guide.md) for details

## Day 4 — Day Trip
Options:
- **Kutná Hora** — Sedlec Ossuary, St. Barbara's Cathedral (~1h by train)
- **Český Krumlov** — medieval town, castle (~2.5–3h by train/bus, best as a full day)

## Day 5 (optional) — Relaxed / Nightlife
- Keep this day intentionally unscheduled
- Explore Vinohrady or Karlín for a lower-key, more local bar scene than Old Town
- Optional: Vyšehrad park and fortress for a quiet morning

## Interests covered
- Historic sights & castle ✅ (Days 1–2)
- Food & beer culture ✅ (throughout, plus Day 3)
- Day trips ✅ (Day 4)
- Nightlife & relaxed pace ✅ (Day 5)
