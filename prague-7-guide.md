# Prague 7 Guide (Holešovice / Letná)

Prague 7 covers the **Holešovice** and **Letná** neighborhoods — a riverside, less-touristy district known for parks, contemporary art, and an industrial-chic character. Worth a dedicated half/full day.

## Sights & culture
- **Letná Park (Letenské sady)** — large hilltop park with sweeping views over Charles Bridge and the Vltava; home to the giant **Metronome** (site of the former Stalin monument).
- **Veletržní palác (Trade Fair Palace)** — National Gallery's collection of 19th–21st century and modern/contemporary art (Picasso, Klimt, Czech modernists).
- **DOX Centre for Contemporary Art** — leading contemporary art space with rotating exhibitions, includes the "Gulliver" airship installation.
- **Výstaviště Praha** — historic exhibition grounds; hosts events, the **Křižík Fountain** (illuminated fountain shows), and occasional markets/festivals.

## Food & drink
- **Letná beer garden** (Nad Letenským sadem) — classic outdoor beer garden in Letná Park, great sunset views over the river.
- **Holešovice Farmers' Market** (Jateční/Výstaviště area) — Saturday morning market with local produce, food stalls.
- Local cafés and restaurants along **Milady Horákové** and around **Vltavská** — generally more local prices than Old Town.

## Nightlife
- **Cross Club** — well-known industrial/alternative music club with distinctive steampunk-style decor.
- A handful of smaller local bars around Holešovice, less touristy than Old Town/Wenceslas Square.

## Getting there
- Metro line C to **Vltavská** or **Nádraží Holešovice**, or trams along Veletržní/Dukelských hrdinů.
- Suggested walking route: Vltavská → Veletržní palác → DOX → Výstaviště → Letná Park (beer garden, Metronome view) → back down toward Malá Strana via the Letná stairs.
